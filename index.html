Advancing Smart Cities across ASEAN and Beyond

This platform is developed by the Digital Economy Promotion Agency (DEPA) and the Smart City Thailand Office, under the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society of Thailand — a contribution to the ASEAN Smart Cities Network.

This is Version 1 — a prototype. The data model, the city profiles, the project evidence table, the essay: these are the first iteration of something intended to grow. The goal is a platform where member cities communicate not only through annual meetings, but through a shared, living record of what is being built, what is working, and what is not. Where interested parties — researchers, practitioners, city officials from outside the network — can follow what ASEAN is learning and find their own entry point. Where the public can understand, in concrete terms, what smart city development in this region actually looks like.

ASEAN has the scale, the diversity of context, and the institutional structure to lead the world in demonstrating what urban transformation looks like when it takes seriously both the technology and the people it is supposed to serve. This platform is one small argument that the record of that work should be public — and that an open record, built on, is how a regional network eventually becomes a global reference.

Data & Privacy

This platform handles public information only. No personal data is collected, stored, or processed through this site. The ASCN contact list referenced in the Contacts section is cited as a bibliographic source for official role designations; names and titles of public officials acting in their official capacity are treated as public information consistent with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) B.E. 2562 and GDPR principles. This platform does not use tracking cookies or analytics. Confidential correspondence should be directed through official ASEAN Secretariat channels. For questions about data handling, write to the platform team at nonsmartcity@gmail.com.
